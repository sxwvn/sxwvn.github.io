This is not a showcase nor a journal entry. This is the essay I hinted on my ABCD release post. Let me start this post frankly by saying I have enough. Enough of this meaningless existence. 

Like a lot of people I grow up having hopes and dreams. The things I want to do, the kind of people I want to become, the values I aligned myself with, all that goofy stuffs. I suppose I worked hard to get what I want and in a sense we're all working hard to get what we want. However lately I come to the conclusion that life is inherently a stochastic machine that will not guarantee you a win even if you work super hard on what you want. Some people cope by thinking this isn't the end of the hero's journey, that it's only a detour that might've saved the hero from a dangerous path. Some people cope by believing that they simply lack the discipline their *successful* peer have, so they keep digging. Me on the other hand, just cope by becoming a miserable nihilist lol.

Hey, you can't fully blame me on that one. I mean, what do you expect when everyone ignored your climb but took notice of your fall? So maybe I *shouldn't* be playing a game that satisfies this people. Maybe all their praises mean nothing and that I could be whoever the fuck I want to be or do what I want to do even if they have no immediate reward. In a sense, nothing matters! I thought I was fucking clever when I thought of that. 

I guess it was easier said than done, something something about **Optimistic Nihilism** or stuff. To be frank, it's been hard for me to be optimistic nowadays, so only the nihilism part stuck with me. 

But maybe it's kinda harsh to judge the meaning of life from a few unfortunate events, right? At least I get to do all the cool and meaningful stuffs to be put on my resume earlier this year, right? Yeah, or maybe everyone will also get a limited amount of "luck" that they can get in a year (or a lifetime?). And I've run out of it so now all I got is just loss after loss after loss. Hmm. But maybe that's not fair, to look at life through a lens of win/lose ratio. I don't think life is as simple as that. Frankly if that's the reason why you're here right now --if you pick that job or study that major to impress someone or to better your chance in a win/lose life-- then I think you're kind of miserable. I mean, listen to yourself out loud, man. You live to compete, that must be tiring. 

At the end of the day, I guess none of these really matter because the country I am from is in total chaos right now. I have bigger things to worry about and my self worth issue clearly isn't one of them.
