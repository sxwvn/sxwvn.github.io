It must be July... Every July for the past 3 years except this one, I go back to my hometown reluctantly. Few things are good about it, like the familiarity. I get to meet my old friends, but that's it. July also means the worst period of your life if you're a freshman just entering high school and higher education. 

I'll keep this entry short, or else more OSINT clues about my life will be out there on the internet (this video is FULL of them), but yelling at 15 y.o kids to greet and welcome them isn't the best idea out there, isn't it? And even long after you graduated, for some odd reasons you still want to come back to that school. But now, you're not the one getting yelled at on the bottom floor, you're up there on the third or fourth. You're power tripping on teenagers when you're on your last few semesters of your university life. I don't know, maybe work on that **Bachelor's Thesis** instead? Or your **Capstone project**? Or get an **internship**? If you wanna meet up with your old buddies you could've just, I don't know, done a meet up in one of their houses? 

You see why I dislike this month and my hometown now?
