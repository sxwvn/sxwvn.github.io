The person behind this project believes that our personal data has been successfully commodified by the Big Tech monopoly. This has lead to consequences ranging from unfunny to un-democratic. The unfunny part being the fact that every small footprint of our digital existance being used to follow us around, and all of that for marketing purposes. 

"We care about your privacy, please allow some cookies" or whatever they say nowadays. 

I build this website to have as little trackers as possible. I don't even have a dedicated analytics module for this site. By the way, it's quite easy to set up your own Github Pages site! I made this one from a template called Bear I think. 

Anyway, the un-democratic consequence is even worse. It started from social media algorithms that reward engagement over truth and civil discussions. No wonder the internet is full of polarized angry mob. And then, generative AI makes everything so much worse by giving these lunatics capabilities of just making shit up quickly and more realistically. I'm talking about deep fakes, obviously. Only a matter of time 'till somebody that dislikes you generate an image of you carrying out a crime you haven't committed.  

I love the internet.
